excerpt: 'A scalable end-to-end pipeline that turns publicly available, in-the-wild instructional videos into high-quality computer-use-agent trajectories without human intervention. ScreenTutor searches and preprocesses online videos at scale, filters irrelevant segments, detects transitions between stable screen states, and identifies the actions performed between them by combining video context, narration, and inverse-dynamics reasoning with vision-language models, yielding ScreenTutorNet, a large-scale dataset of 14.2K multimodal trajectories.'
